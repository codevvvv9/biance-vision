## The research question

An invented range contains repeated reversals. Is there enough evidence to prefer one direction now? The answer in this case is no. “Wait” describes insufficient directional evidence at the snapshot, not a prediction that price will stay still or an instruction about an existing position.

The synthetic chart shows overlap and unsuccessful attempts to sustain direction. There are no live quotes or historical returns in this case. The key observation is unresolved balance, not a hidden forecast about the next move.

## Why a stronger conclusion is unsupported

Both sides have failed to sustain a move, and overlapping observations make a simple continuation story fragile. Choosing a side would conceal the contradiction rather than resolve it. The opposing possibility remains that a new move can begin before confirmation; a cautious interpretation can miss an early move.

## A condition for revisiting

Revisit after a sustained departure from the range and a newly confirmed structure, using a declared horizon. First verify the fresh data instead of reusing the old snapshot. If the feed is missing, state that limitation: no data is different from balanced evidence. Neither condition justifies a fabricated probability.

## Carry the question forward

A useful next question is: “What evidence is still missing, and which observable change would justify a new assessment?” The terminal can help organize that investigation after account access is enabled. Choosing a public example does not automatically start research or spend an account allowance.
