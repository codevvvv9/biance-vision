## The research question

An invented upward structure is pulling back toward a prior breakout area. Does that preserve a plausible continuation thesis, or is the structure changing? The conditional answer is to wait for a response at the area; the location alone is insufficient. No real market or subsequent outcome is being asserted.

## Supporting evidence and the opposing case

Higher swing lows preserve the upward backdrop, and the return to a prior breakout area gives a specific place to observe the response. Against that, overlapping recent candles suggest indecision. Reaching an area does not demonstrate decisive buying or rule out a deeper reversal. These observations carry different implications, not equal votes.

## What would change the view

A sustained break below the relevant structural low would weaken the continuation thesis and require a fresh snapshot. A stronger response at the contested zone could support further investigation, but does not guarantee follow-through. The meaning of “sustained” must be specified for a real research horizon; this teaching case does not provide an order trigger.
