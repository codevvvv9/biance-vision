## An independent research project

DuckStrat is an independently maintained crypto market research project. It connects market context, price action, supporting evidence and opposing evidence so that a user can examine a conditional interpretation. It is not a trading exchange, brokerage or portfolio custodian.

Duck means staying nimble around risk and cognitive traps. Strat is short for strategy: a view should be built on evidence, challenged by counterevidence and bounded by conditions. Waiting for confirmation is a valid conclusion. The name does not imply that market risk can be eliminated.

## Who prepares these notes

The DuckStrat project maintainers are responsible for the product and its public notes. Each note is checked against the linked sources and the current implementation, and revised as the product evolves.

## Market data does not imply affiliation

The terminal uses public Binance market data for supported workflows. DuckStrat is not affiliated with, endorsed by or operated by Binance. Exchange documentation explains data and product terms; it does not endorse our interpretations.

## What access provides

Supported workflows include market-structure research, perpetual-position review and research using explicitly entered event-quote terms. Available data, model configuration and account limits determine what a particular run can do. The tool does not connect a trading account or execute orders. Public diagrams are teaching illustrations, not live recommendations.
