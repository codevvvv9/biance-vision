## An independent research project

Biance Vision is an independently maintained crypto market research project. It connects market context, price action, supporting evidence and opposing evidence so that a user can examine a conditional interpretation. It is not a trading exchange, brokerage or portfolio custodian.

## Who prepares these notes

The Biance Vision project maintainers are responsible for the product and its public notes. These notes were prepared with AI assistance and checked against the linked sources and the current implementation. This is a technical editorial check, not an independent financial review or a claim of professional accreditation. No named analyst, institutional affiliation or verified performance record is claimed.

## Market data does not imply affiliation

The terminal uses public Binance market data for supported workflows. Biance Vision is not affiliated with, endorsed by or operated by Binance. The similar project name and references to exchange products do not imply a partnership. Exchange documentation explains data and product terms; it does not endorse our interpretations.

## What access provides

Supported workflows include market-structure research, perpetual-position review and research using explicitly entered event-quote terms. Available data, model configuration and account limits determine what a particular run can do. The tool does not connect a trading account or execute orders. Public diagrams are teaching illustrations, not live recommendations.

## Access and geographic boundaries

The terminal is intended for paid research use; access is currently arranged manually. No unannounced price, free allowance or response time is promised. The public website is globally accessible, while terminal and login access currently restrict mainland China. Confirm supported use before obtaining an account. Contact the project through the access page; existing account holders can log in directly.
